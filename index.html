      Scenery and quality changes apply on reload.

      Controls

      Gamepad: left stick = pitch/roll, right stick = rudder, triggers = throttle, A gear, X/Y flaps, B speedbrake, LB brakes, RB autopilot. Joysticks / HOTAS: X/Y axes = roll/pitch, throttle axis, twist = rudder.

      Quick start

      1. Release the parking brake with N, set takeoff flaps with F (one or two notches).
      2. Hold W for full thrust. At the "Rotate" call, hold ↓ gently to lift the nose ~10–15°.
      3. Positive climb: gear up G, retract flaps R as you accelerate.
      4. Engage the autopilot with Z or click the panel (CMD), set HDG/ALT/SPD with the MCP buttons.
      5. To land: press APP on the MCP near the runway, extend flaps and gear, arm spoilers (Shift+/).
